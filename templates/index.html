🛡️ How Your Medical Privacy is Guaranteed

Unlike traditional cloud healthcare apps where personal telemetry is uploaded to remote servers, HealthShield uses Decentralized Federated Learning with Output Differential Privacy.

1

Zero Raw Data Leaves Your Device

All sensor readings (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen) stay locked strictly inside your local edge storage. No remote database ever stores your vitals.

2

Local Mathematical Learning

Your local machine trains a lightweight model on your personal data to extract mathematical gradients and weights without exposing individual records.

3

Differential Privacy Laplace Noise Mask (ε=1.0)

Before transmitting model weights, mathematical Laplace noise calibrated to sensitivity R_clip=1.5 is added. This makes it mathematically impossible to reverse-engineer your vitals.

4

Global Model Aggregation (FedAvg)

The central coordinator merges noised updates across all client nodes to build a collective health intelligence that benefits everyone.
